# Comparability

The registry assesses definition, unit, scope, population, period, exclusions and aggregation method. Statuses are `comparable`, `comparable_after_unit_conversion`, `comparable_after_scope_review`, `label_only`, `unresolved` and `not_comparable`.

The engine blocks calculations for unresolved, label-only, non-comparable and scope-review inputs. Override YAML requires a decision ID and rationale; the matrix records the decision, the analyst bundle writes `overrides.json`, and the warnings report calls out each applied override.

Lakh ÷ 100 is a mechanical conversion to crore, and years × 12 is a mechanical conversion to months. Neither proves that principal outstanding, consideration received, loan amount assigned, maturity or residual maturity have the same economic meaning.
